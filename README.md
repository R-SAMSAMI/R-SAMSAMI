# Reihaneh (Rei) Samsami

**I build AI systems for construction and infrastructure, and the governance that keeps them trustworthy: inspection copilots, document intelligence, BIM data pipelines, and the evaluation and monitoring that make agentic AI safe to ship.**

Most AI in this industry breaks on the same thing. The inputs are site photos, scanned spec books, agency standards, and Revit schedule exports that were never meant to be machine-readable, and the outputs get handed to an inspector or a superintendent who is accountable for them. I work on the layers in between: extraction, grounding, structured output, and the evaluation, guardrails, and drift monitoring that have to exist before any of it is safe to ship.

**Responsible AI and agentic governance.** I apply the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage) and the EU AI Act to real agentic systems: evaluation and reliability testing, human-in-the-loop oversight, logging and drift detection, and incident escalation. Governance built into the system, not bolted on.

**Featured sample: [Agent Output Watchdog](https://github.com/R-SAMSAMI/agent-output-watchdog) · [live demo](https://rei-agent-output-watchdog.streamlit.app/).** A runtime watchdog for an LLM/RAG agent that scores every response for grounding, logs each run to Delta Lake, detects anomalies and distribution drift (PSI), and opens incidents to a human-review queue when quality slips. `Python · LangChain · Delta Lake · Streamlit`
